# Sky photos (HDRIs)

What the cars reflect and the ambient light come from these real skies. All four
are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are **CC0** (public domain):
free to use for anything, no attribution required (credited here anyway).

| File | Poly Haven asset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `quarry_01.exr` | Quarry 01 | clear days |
| `venice_sunset.exr` | Venice Sunset | low sun, evening races |
| `blouberg_sunrise_2.exr` | Blouberg Sunrise 2 | overcast and rain |
| `moonless_golf.exr` | Moonless Golf | night races |

They came via the three.js repository's example textures and were scaled to
512 x 256 (half-float EXR) to keep the web download small.
