# Race-day props

From **Racing Kit** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit), licensed
Creative Commons Zero (CC0): see `License.txt`. Used as-is (glTF binary), scaled
and placed by `scripts/props.gd`:

| Model | Used as | Real size in game |
|---|---|---|
| `tent.glb`, `tentLong.glb` | team tents in the paddock | 3.5 m / 7 m across |
| `pylon.glb` | cones along the paddock walkway | 0.7 m tall |
| `billboard.glb` (+ `billboard_tankcoBanner.png`) | sponsor boards outside the backstretch | 9 m wide |
| `radarEquipment.glb` | TV satellite dish by the media centre | about 5 m |
| `barrierRed.glb`, `barrierWhite.glb` | barriers lining road-course corners | 1.5 m sections |
